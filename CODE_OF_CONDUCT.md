# Community code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent while discussing ideas,
but take responsibility for impact. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized attention, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Keep technical criticism about the work. Respect privacy and do not share another
person's data or private communications without permission. Maintainers may edit,
hide, lock, or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to keep the
community safe and useful.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do
not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be
reviewed as promptly and impartially as maintainer capacity permits.
